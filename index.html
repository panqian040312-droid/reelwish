Swap models, not integrations

Your client points at https://reelwish.cc/v1 and stays there. Upstream endpoints and upstream credentials never enter your codebase.

POST /v1/videos
Request
curl -X POST https://reelwish.cc/v1/videos \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk-••••" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "seedance-2-0-special",
    "prompt": "A cinematic city at night",
    "mode": "text_to_video",
    "seconds": "5",
    "aspect_ratio": "16:9",
    "resolution": "720p"
  }'
Response · 200 ok
{
  "task_id": "task_…",
  "status": "submitted",
  "estimated_cost_usd": 0.25
}

Under the hood

What the gateway handles for you

Everything between your request and the upstream provider — so a bad minute on their side doesn't become a bad day on yours.

Load balancing

Requests spread across upstream services instead of piling onto one.

Rate limiting & retries

Scheduling controls between you and the provider, so a busy upstream doesn't fail your task outright.

Cost tracking

Real-time usage monitoring and per-task USD charges you can audit.

Transparent billing

Pay-as-you-go, priced per task. No monthly subscription, no unused credits.

Multi-region delivery

Deployed across regions for stable global access.

Team collaboration

Multi-user management with flexible permission allocation.
